ReaderCount shows you who reads your website, without cookies and without tracking anyone.

See today at a glance: visitors, page views, time on page and clicks on your links, with a chart by the hour and a comparison with yesterday.

Watch readers arrive live, with the pages they're on and where they came from.

Follow visitor trails to see the path each reader takes through your site, and the journeys that come up most often.

Find out where readers come from: Google, social sites, newsletters and campaign links, plus the countries and towns they read from.

Site Health checks your pages for broken links, missing titles and other problems, and gives you ways to improve. Mark each one as done when it's fixed.

Get alerts when traffic spikes or stops, when a link breaks, or when a page passes a number you choose.

Widgets on your Home Screen and a live count in the Mac menu bar keep your numbers in view.

ReaderCount counts visits without cookies, so your readers never see a consent banner. You'll need a ReaderCount account and the ReaderCount script on your site. Settings and plans are managed on readercount.com.
